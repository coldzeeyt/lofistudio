# Bundled music provenance

All 30 bundled tracks are by **HoliznaCC0 / Holizna**, downloaded on 2026-10-12 from the artist's OpenGameArt submissions. The submission pages identify the author and release the music under **CC0 1.0 Universal**, permitting copying, modification and redistribution, including commercial use.

Primary source pages:

- [Lo-Fi and Chill — three archives](https://opengameart.org/content/lo-fi-and-chill-collection)
- [Sad Lo-Fi](https://opengameart.org/content/sad-lo-fi-lofi-collectioni)
- [Happy Lo-Fi](https://opengameart.org/content/happy-lo-fi-lofi-collection)
- [CC0 dedication](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- [CC0 legal text](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode)

CC0 does not require attribution. Lofi Studio nonetheless includes the artist, track titles, dedication and source links in generated YouTube descriptions. It does not imply artist endorsement. Licensing does not guarantee immunity from erroneous automated Content ID claims.

`manifest.json` records, for every track: the precise archive URL and member filename, original SHA-256 hash, bundled file hash, source page, license URL, duration and transcoding notes. The bundled files are stereo 192 kbps MP3 at 48 kHz. Export processing normalizes loudness, repeats and crossfades the music.

Tracks are included as actual audio files under `audio/`, so generation has no runtime dependency on a third-party music provider. No subscription, API key or paid music service is used.
